Full body. Leg press holds at 58 kg — this is a position test, not a load week.

The right glute spoke at depth on 9 Sep at this load, and the fix you identified — handles held, back and glutes pinned to the pad through the range — has not been tested. Stepping to 65 kg today would test position and load in the same set. Hold 58 at 12 reps, confirm the range is quiet, and Wednesday takes the load step.

Upper lifts step on reps, not load. Chest press to 10, both pulls to 12. At 35 kg the next machine rung is 42 kg, which sits at 89% of e1RM on all three — reps carry these for a while yet.

Bulgarian split squat steps to 10 kg/hand at the same 10 reps. Control at ten is proven across three sessions, and extending reps on this movement lengthens the fatigue window where right knee collapse appears. 10 kg/hand is also the last fine rung before the 20 kg pair.
Target 2-3 RIR on load-progressed lifts — sits on legs that ran long; upper lifts in this session take the upper band. On split squats RIR is logged with knee tracking held — stop on tracking, not on reps left. Planks and dead bugs are prescribed sets.
Warm-Up — ~10 min

Full body means the shoulders and the hips both get loaded — prime both, briefly, rather than doing either warm-up in full. Split squats come late in the session, so the single-leg prep matters.

01
Ankle circles — 10 each direction, each foot. First, given the Achilles history.
02
Shoulder rolls + arm circles — 30 s. Raise the temperature before the press and pulldown.
03
Glute bridges — 2 × 10. Drive hips up, squeeze hard at the top.
04
Scapular wall slides — 8–10. Goalpost arms, hands and elbows on the wall.
05
Bodyweight squats — 10 slow, parallel or below. Rehearses the leg press pattern.
06
Hip circles / single-leg balance — 10 per leg, or 30 s balance per leg. Rehearses the split-squat stance — the right side is the one to feel.
07
Calf raises (bodyweight) — 2 × 8 slow, 3 s down. Prep for the eccentric work later.
Exercises
01 / 07
Leg Press
Life Fitness Optima · OSLP · 3 × 12 @ 58 kg · Rest 90–120 sec
Seated horizontal press — quads first, glutes and hamstrings assisting, no spinal load.
Quick reset
  • Back flush; knees ~90° at start; whole foot on the platform; latches engaged.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Push the floor away — glutes pinned to the seat, don't lift the hips.
  • Knees track over the toes — don't let the right knee cave inward under fatigue. That's your old collapse pattern; the late reps are where it shows.
  • Soft lockout, 2 s back.
58 kg is the working target. If set 1 sits at RPE ≤5, hold today and we step up next session; if form breaks or the right knee caves, drop a pin. Log the weight + RPE.

Quick reset

03 / 07
Lat Pulldown
Life Fitness Optima · OSLR · 3 × 12 @ 35 kg · Rest 90 sec
Vertical pull — lats first, with biceps and rear delts assisting. The movement pattern the previous block had no exercise for.
Quick reset
  • Thigh pad snug; grip just outside shoulders; ~10° lean, chest up.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Elbows to the back pockets — you're pulling with the lats, not curling with the arms.
  • Chest up to meet the bar rather than leaning back to drag it down.
  • Full stretch at the top is where the growth is — don't shorten the rep to add weight.
35 kg is the working target. New movement — expect to recalibrate after set 1. If you're leaning back past ~20° to move it, drop a pin.
04 / 07
Seated Low Row
Life Fitness Optima · OSLR · 3 × 12 @ 35 kg · Rest 90 sec
Bilateral horizontal pull on the same frame as the lat pulldown — flat bench, ankle rollers, shared 91 kg stack. This is the heavy mid-back builder: rhomboids, mid-traps and lats through a long range, progressing in 7 kg steps.
Quick reset
  • Feet on the rollers, soft knees; chest tall, flat back; taut cable at full reach.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Elbows past the ribs — the hands are hooks, the back does the work.
  • The torso stays still. If you're rowing with the lower back, drop a pin.
  • Full stretch at the front, no rounding — the lengthened position is where the growth is.
Shares one stack with the lat pulldown, so run them back-to-back at the same station. The pulldown pin and the row pin will not be the same number — most people row heavier.
35 kg is executed, not a seed — proven 21 Aug at 3×8. Build to 12 across all three sets before the next 7 kg step. Log reps + RPE.

Single-leg squat with the rear foot elevated — the most running-specific lift here, with high demand on hip stability and glute strength.

06 / 07
Leg Extension
Life Fitness Optima · OSLEC · 3 × 15 @ 14 kg · Rest 75–90 sec
Isolated quad work with 91 kg of headroom. Complements the leg press rather than replacing it — the press is the compound driver.
Quick reset
  • Back flush; knee on the pivot; shin pad just above the ankle.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Squeeze at the top rather than kicking into the end range.
  • Both legs extending evenly — watch for the right leg lagging or the knee drifting inward.
  • Never bounce out of the bottom.
14 kg is the working target. New movement — recalibrate after set 1.
07 / 07
Copenhagen Plank
Bodyweight · 3 × 30 s/side BW · Rest 90 sec
Side-plank loading the hip adductors under a long lever — one of the best-evidenced groin-stability exercises for runners, and direct carry-over from your 2021 rehab.
Quick reset
  • Forearm down, top foot/ankle on a bench; knee version if the hold gets sloppy.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
Key cues
  • Push the surface away — the active drive is what loads the adductor.
  • Stack the hips — squared hips keep the right side out of its collapse pattern.
  • Quality over the clock — a clean shorter hold beats a sagging long one.
Sharp groin/adductor pain or a notably weaker right side — shorten the hold or use the knee version, and log it.
Cool-Down — ~8–10 min

You pressed, pulled and squatted — this covers both halves. Calf and Achilles first, as always after loaded knee work.

01
Calf + soleus stretch (wall) — 45 s straight-knee then 30 s bent-knee per leg. Both sides.
02
Standing quad stretch — 45 s per leg. The split squats will have found these.
03
Doorway / pec stretch — 45 s per side. Opens the pecs after the chest press.
04
Lat stretch — 30 s per side. Eases the upper back after the pulldown and row.
05
Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch — 45–90 s per side. Today's daily hip-flexor work — ticked off.
06
Figure-4 / pigeon — 60 s per side. Opens the hip rotators.
