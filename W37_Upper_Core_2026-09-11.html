Warm-Up — ~8 min

Wake the shoulders and upper back before you press, row and curl. Then do each machine's own light set.

01
Shoulder rolls + arm circles — 30 s. Raise the temperature, loosen the joint.
02
Prone 'T' raises — 2 × 12 slow, face-down on the bench, thumbs up. Squeeze the shoulder blades. No bands in this gym — this is the primary, not the substitute. A light cable face-pull on the DAP works too.
03
Towel pass-throughs — 8–10. Wide grip overhead and back, arms straight. Opens the shoulders for pressing.
04
Scapular wall slides — 8–10. Goalpost arms, slide up and down keeping hands and elbows on the wall.
05
Dead bug (unloaded) — 5 per side. Primes the trunk for the plank.
Exercises
01 / 08
Machine Chest Press
Life Fitness Optima · OSCP · 3 × 8 @ 35 kg · Rest 90 sec
Seated press for chest, front shoulders and triceps, path guided by the machine.
Quick reset
  • Back flush; handles at mid-chest; elbows ~45°.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Blades down and back — don't shrug toward the ears.
  • Elbows at 45° — tucked is stronger and easier on the shoulder.
  • Soft lockout, 2 s back.
Reps before pins: hit 8 clean at 35 kg across all three sets before adding a pin. Log reps + RPE.
02 / 08
Seated Low Row
Life Fitness Optima · OSLR · 3 × 10 @ 35 kg · Rest 90 sec
Bilateral horizontal pull on the same frame as the lat pulldown — flat bench, ankle rollers, shared 91 kg stack. This is the heavy mid-back builder: rhomboids, mid-traps and lats through a long range, progressing in 7 kg steps.
Quick reset
  • Feet on the rollers, soft knees; chest tall, flat back; taut cable at full reach.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Elbows past the ribs — the hands are hooks, the back does the work.
  • The torso stays still. If you're rowing with the lower back, drop a pin.
  • Full stretch at the front, no rounding — the lengthened position is where the growth is.
Shares one stack with the lat pulldown, so run them back-to-back at the same station. The pulldown pin and the row pin will not be the same number — most people row heavier.
35 kg is executed, not a seed — proven 21 Aug at 3×8. Build to 10 across all three sets before the next 7 kg step. Log reps + RPE.
03 / 08
Lat Pulldown
Life Fitness Optima · OSLR · 3 × 10 @ 35 kg · Rest 90 sec
Vertical pull — lats first, with biceps and rear delts assisting. The movement pattern the previous block had no exercise for.
Quick reset
  • Thigh pad snug; grip just outside shoulders; ~10° lean, chest up.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Elbows to the back pockets — you're pulling with the lats, not curling with the arms.
  • Chest up to meet the bar rather than leaning back to drag it down.
  • Full stretch at the top is where the growth is — don't shorten the rep to add weight.
35 kg is the working target. New movement — expect to recalibrate after set 1. If you're leaning back past ~20° to move it, drop a pin.
04 / 08
Machine Shoulder Press
Life Fitness Optima · OSSP · 3 × 15 @ 14 kg · Rest 90 sec
Seated overhead press — shoulders and triceps — path guided by the machine. The work arm is counter-balanced, so the lowest pins feel light; climb to find a real working weight.
Quick reset
  • Back to the pad; handles at shoulder height; straight wrists.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Ribs down — press from the shoulders, don't arch the lower back.
  • No shrug — keep the neck long.
  • Soft lockout — stop just short of straight.
Counter-balanced, so the pin number reads light. 14 kg is the established working weight now, not a starting guess — hold it and build to 15 on all three sets. 7 kg is the smallest step on this stack, so it's a real jump when it comes. Log reps + RPE.
05 / 08
Machine Bicep Curl
Life Fitness Optima · OSBC · 3 × 15 @ 14 kg · Rest 60–90 sec
Seated curl with the upper arms on a pad — isolates the biceps and brachialis. Counter-balanced and a smaller stack (tops out at 63 kg).
Quick reset
  • Upper arms flat on the pad; self-aligning handles; soft start.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Arms stay on the pad.
  • Full range — squeeze top, control the bottom.
  • 2 s down — the lowering builds the muscle.
Counter-balanced — work where 15 reps leaves ~2 in reserve. Log the pin.
06 / 08
Overhead Cable Triceps Extension
Dual Adjustable Pulley · OSDAP · 3 × 8 @ 10 kg · Rest 60–90 sec
Rope on the cable, facing away, pressing overhead from behind the head — loads the triceps in the stretched position. Balances the pressing work.
Quick reset
  • Rope high, face away, elbows forward and high; ribs down.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Only the forearms move — elbows stay forward and quiet.
  • Ribs down — don't arch to press.
  • Full stretch at the bottom, 2 s.
07 / 08
Pallof Press
Dual Adjustable Pulley · OSDAP · 3 × 10/side @ 10 kg · Rest 60 sec
Anti-rotation press on the cable column. The cable tries to twist you and you refuse — the same job the trunk does every stride to stop the right hip dropping.
Quick reset
  • Pulley at sternum height; side-on, feet hip-width, cable taut.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
RIR
Key cues
  • Ribs down, glutes on — brace before the first press.
  • No hip shift — the pelvis stays square through every rep.
  • Slow out, slow back — speed hides the rotation you're meant to resist.
If the hips rotate or you lean away to finish the press, the load is too heavy. Drop 2.5 kg rather than shifting.
08 / 08
Dead Bug
Bodyweight · 3 × 12 BW · Rest 60 sec
Anti-extension core — the trunk holds steady while the limbs drive, the same job it does each stride.
Quick reset
  • On your back, tabletop legs, lower back pressed flat throughout.
Tap when done
RepsLoad
Key cues
  • Back flat like a table.
  • Slow — 3 s out, 2 s back, opposite arm and leg.
Cool-Down — ~6–8 min

Targets what you just worked — chest, shoulders, upper back, arms — plus today's daily hip-flexor stretch.

01
Doorway / pec stretch — 45 s per side. Opens the pecs after pressing.
02
Cross-body shoulder + overhead triceps — 30 s and 20 s per side.
03
Lat stretch — 30 s per side. Eases the upper back after the rows.
04
Thoracic extension / child's pose with reach — 45 s. Decompress after the bracing.
05
Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch — 45–90 s per side. Today's daily hip-flexor work — ticked off.
